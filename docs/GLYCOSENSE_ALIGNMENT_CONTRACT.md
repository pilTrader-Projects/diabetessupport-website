## 4. Approved Clinical Boundaries & Disclaimers

### A. Approved Health Claims
- **Target Glucose Range**: Standard non-pregnant clinical target of **70–140 mg/dL** (based on ADA standards).
- **Blood Pressure Standards**: Categorized according to American Heart Association (AHA) guidelines (Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2, Crisis).
- **Risk Assessment**: Scientifically validated Finnish Diabetes Risk Score (FINDRISC) for 10-year Type 2 diabetes risk estimation.
- **AI Health Coach Demeanor**: Reflective, non-judgmental habit support focused on pattern awareness rather than medical prescription.

### B. Strictly Prohibited Claims (Over-promising Guardrail)
1. ❌ **NEVER** claim GlycoSense "cures", "reverses", or "diagnoses" diabetes or any chronic medical condition.
2. ❌ **NEVER** claim GlycoSense is an "FDA-approved medical device" or CGM hardware manufacturer (it is a software tracking and decision-support tool).
3. ❌ **NEVER** advertise cloud synchronization of biometric data as a default (privacy is local-first).
4. ❌ **NEVER** give prescriptive insulin dosing advice through marketing copy or lead magnets.

### C. Mandatory Copywriting Disclaimer
Every external publication, landing page, and lead magnet created by the Advocacy Project must display:
> *"GlycoSense is an educational wellness tracking and decision-support application. It does not provide medical diagnosis or replace consultation with a licensed physician or endocrinologist. Always consult your healthcare provider before adjusting medications or dietary regimens."*
