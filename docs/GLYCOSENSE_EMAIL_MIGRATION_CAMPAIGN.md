## 📧 Email 2: The 2-Minute Migration Guide (2 Weeks Before Cutover)

**Subject:** 📲 2-Minute Guide: Transfer your GlycoSense records to the new app  
**Preview Text:** Bring your glucose history, meals, and meds with you in 3 easy steps.  

Hi {{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }},

Our official new home is live! You can now transfer all your historical readings, meals, and medications into the new, faster GlycoSense app in just 2 minutes.

### Here is the 3-step transfer:

1. **Step 1: Download your backup on your current app**  
   - Open your current GlycoSense app.
   - Go to **Settings (Profile icon) → Data Management**.
   - Tap **"Download Full Backup (JSON)"**. A file containing your records will save to your phone or computer.

2. **Step 2: Open our new home**  
   - Navigate to our official domain: **[https://YOUR-CUSTOM-DOMAIN.com]** on your phone or browser.

3. **Step 3: Restore your records**  
   - In the new app, sign in or go to **Settings → Data Management**.
   - Under *Restore from Backup*, tap **"Choose Backup File (.json)"** and select the file you just downloaded.
   - Tap **Confirm** — and you're done! All your historical charts and logs will immediately appear on your dashboard.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you see any duplicates, don't worry! Our restore engine automatically skips identical readings to keep your charts clean.

### If you are on an iPhone (Safari):
1. Open **[https://YOUR-CUSTOM-DOMAIN.com]** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share button** (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).

1. Open **[https://YOUR-CUSTOM-DOMAIN.com]** in Chrome.

3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**.
