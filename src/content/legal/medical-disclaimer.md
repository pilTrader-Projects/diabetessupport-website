## ⚠️ Mandatory Emergency & Non-Diagnostic Notice

**If you are experiencing a medical emergency, severe hypoglycemia, ketoacidosis, acute chest pain, shortness of breath, or sudden loss of consciousness, do NOT rely on this website. Call your local emergency medical services or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department.**
:::

## 1. Educational Purpose Only (No Doctor-Patient Relationship)

All materials, protocols, video curations, data visualizations, calculators, and lifestyle observations published on **Before the Numbers** ({{siteUrl}}) are provided solely for **general health awareness and educational purposes**.

- **No Medical Advice:** Nothing contained on this website constitutes professional medical diagnosis, individualized clinical advice, therapeutic prescription, or disease treatment.
- **No Physician-Patient Relationship:** Using this website, reading guides, participating in community threads, or utilizing local logbook tools does NOT establish a physician-patient, dietitian-patient, or healthcare provider relationship with Before the Numbers, its founders, authors, or medical reviewers.

## 2. Medication Warnings & Fasting Precautions

Adjusting lifestyle habits, carbohydrate intake, or meal frequency can have immediate, potent physiological effects on blood glucose levels and insulin sensitivity:

- **Prescription Medication Warning:** If you take prescription medications for diabetes (including insulin, sulfonylureas, SGLT2 inhibitors, or GLP-1 receptor agonists) or high blood pressure, **you must NOT adjust or discontinue medications without direct physician supervision**. Rapid changes in diet can precipitate severe hypoglycemia or hypotension.
- **Fasting Precautions:** Extended fasting, dry fasting, or aggressive intermittent fasting regimens are contraindicated for pregnant individuals, breastfeeding mothers, individuals with a history of eating disorders, individuals with Type 1 diabetes, or those with advanced renal impairment unless explicitly authorized and monitored by licensed clinicians.

## 3. Individual Variation & Professional Consultation

Metabolic responses vary dramatically based on genetic predisposition, baseline organ function, microbiome composition, and concurrent medical conditions:

- Always consult a board-certified physician, endocrinologist, or licensed nutritionist-dietitian before adopting new metabolic protocols, exercise regimens, or supplements.
- Diagnostic lab thresholds (such as fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, or lipid panels) referenced on this site represent generalized clinical literature benchmarks and must be interpreted by your personal physician in the context of your comprehensive health history.

## 4. Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) Disclaimer

Companion digital utilities (including GlycoSense):
- Function exclusively as **personal logging notebooks and numerical trend trackers**.
- Do not automate diagnostic decisions, calculate drug dosages, or alert emergency services.
- Data captured stays on your local device (via browser `localStorage`) unless explicitly shared via ephemeral PIN.
