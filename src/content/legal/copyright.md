## 1. Curated Educational Video Embeds

Before the Numbers organizes and curates high-impact metabolic health lectures, interviews, and public domain science:

- **Official Embed Mechanism:** All third-party video presentations are embedded strictly via the **official YouTube embedded player API**.
- **No Hosting or Re-Uploading:** We do **never download, re-encode, rip, or re-host** video files on our private servers. All video streams, view metrics, and ad revenue natively accrue to the original YouTube creator channels.
- **Creator Attribution:** Every cataloged lecture visibly credits the original channel name, lecture title, and direct link back to YouTube.

## 2. Non-Affiliation with Featured Speakers

Featured lectures, podcast episodes, and scientific talks include commentary from independent researchers, physicians, and educators (e.g., Dr. Jason Fung, Dr. Pradip Jamnadas, Dr. Benjamin Bikman, and Dr. Ken Berry):

- **No Endorsement:** Inclusion of educational lectures does **NOT** imply that featured speakers endorse, sponsor, or are affiliated with Before the Numbers or its commercial digital toolkits.
- **No Use of Likeness to Sell:** Before the Numbers strictly adheres to an ethical standard: **we never use a speaker's name, photograph, likeness, or quote to sell, promote, or endorse paid starter packs or commercial affiliate items**.

## 4. DMCA & Copyright Takedown Procedure

We respect the intellectual property of creators worldwide. If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe that content embedded or published on Before the Numbers infringes your copyright, please submit an expedited takedown notice:

- **Required Information:** (1) Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed; (2) The exact URL or slug on Before the Numbers; (3) Your contact details (name, email address, relationship to copyright holder); (4) A statement made in good faith that the disputed use is unauthorized.
- **Takedown Email:** Send requests directly to [copyright@beforethenumbers.org](mailto:copyright@beforethenumbers.org).
- **Turnaround:** Verified takedown requests are investigated and removed within **48 business hours**.
