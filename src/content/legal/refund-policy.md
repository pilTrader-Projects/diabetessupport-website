## 1. Digital Product Refund Eligibility

Before the Numbers publishes digital educational materials, guides, and companion tooling (such as the upcoming metabolic starter pack). Because digital goods are delivered instantly upon completed checkout:

- **Refund Window:** We offer a **7-day satisfaction refund period** from the exact date and timestamp of initial purchase.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** If an educational guide or digital resource does not meet your expectations, or if you encountered technical access difficulties, you may request a full refund within the 7-day window.
- **Proof of Purchase:** Refund requests must include the original transaction reference number and the email address used during checkout via our authorized merchant-of-record platform (e.g., Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy).

## 2. Processing & Payout Timelines

Once an eligible refund request is verified by our administrative team:

- Refunds are credited back to the original method of payment (credit/debit card, e-wallet, or digital payment provider).
- Financial institution processing times typically range between **5 to 10 business days** depending on your local banking institution.
- Transaction processing fees (if non-refundable by the merchant-of-record processor) may be subject to platform terms.

## 3. Exceptions & Abuse Prevention

To prevent fraudulent exploitation of downloadable intellectual property:

- Refund requests initiated after the expiration of the 7-day period are non-refundable.
- Repetitive purchase-and-refund cycles for the same digital asset will be flagged and subject to account restriction.
- Unauthorized chargebacks initiated without prior contact with our support desk will result in revocation of access to all companion tools and downloadable updates.

To initiate a refund or dispute resolution, contact our billing and compliance team:
