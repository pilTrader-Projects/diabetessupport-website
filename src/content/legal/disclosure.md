## 1. How Before the Numbers is Funded

Before the Numbers is an educational movement committed to metabolic health transparency. To maintain our server infrastructure, support full-time curation, and keep our primary health education free for families across Asia, our platform receives funding through:

- **Programmatic Display Advertising:** Google AdSense advertisements displayed across select public articles and guides.
- **Affiliate Partnerships:** Small referral commissions when readers purchase recommended health gear or laboratory products.
- **Direct Educational Products:** Self-published, value-add digital resources (such as starter packs, shopping guides, and meal planning workbooks).
:::

## 2. Google AdSense & Programmatic Advertising

We partner with **Google AdSense** to display contextually relevant advertisements:

- Advertisements are automatically populated by Google's ad network and are clearly marked with standard ad indicators (e.g., "Ad", "Sponsored", or "Google Ads").
- The presence of an advertisement on Before the Numbers does **NOT** constitute an endorsement or recommendation of the advertised product, pharmaceutical, supplement, or medical device.
- Ad networks utilize cookies to personalize and serve programmatic ads based on browsing behavior. Readers may manage consent at any time via our Cookie Settings or opt out via [Google Ads Settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## 3. Affiliate Relationships & Sponsored Links

Certain links published on our learning hub, product recommendations, or community articles are **affiliate links**:

- When you click an affiliate link and complete a purchase through a verified merchant partner (such as Shopee, Lazada, or Amazon), Before the Numbers may earn a small referral commission at **no additional cost to you**.
- All affiliate links are programmatically marked with `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` and are accompanied by an inline or card-level disclosure notice.
- We maintain strict editorial independence: we do not accept payment to promote scam supplements, unverified "cure" concoctions, or diagnostic devices lacking legitimate regulatory approval (such as Philippine FDA registration).

## 4. Editorial Independence & Non-Influence

Commercial relationships, sponsors, and affiliate partners have **zero editorial control** over our research summaries, clinical video curations, or educational critiques:

- Sponsored content (if occasionally published) is prominently flagged with a "Sponsored Content" badge and is strictly barred from our core 5-step curriculum.
- Recommendations are driven by clinical utility, scientific rigor, and patient usability—never by commission rates.
